How it works

Where the money
actually comes from.

Nufaa does not lend, does not charge interest and does not invest your balance. Cashback is a discount a seller agrees to give in exchange for the order. This page shows the whole loop, including the arithmetic.

The loop

Five steps,
then it repeats.

  1. 01

    Verify your identity

    Name, date of birth, address, and a passport or Gambian NID number with a photo of the document and a photo of you. Nufaa is registered in two jurisdictions and moves real money, so this is a legal obligation on us, not a preference.

    Open the KYC form
  2. 02

    Put Dalasi in the wallet

    Mobile money, bank transfer, or cash handed to a listed agent. If your money is in crypto, send USDT, USDC or BTC and we convert it — you see the Dalasi figure and the rate before anything moves, and the rate holds for 60 seconds.

    See the wallet
  3. 03

    Order, or list and sell

    Buyers pick from listed sellers and pay from their wallet balance. Sellers do the mirror image: list stock priced in Dalasi, set a cashback rate between 10% and 20%, and take orders from buyers who have already been verified. The rate a seller picks is the single biggest lever they have on where their listing ranks.

  4. 04

    The order is confirmed

    Goods are delivered or collected at an agent point. The seller marks the order complete, or it completes automatically after the collection window closes. Nothing is released before that, which is what keeps disputes rare.

  5. 05

    Cashback is credited in Dalasi

    The seller's commission is taken, your cashback is split out of it, and the remainder settles to the seller. Your share appears in your wallet as ordinary Dalasi — no points, no separate reward balance, no expiry date. Spend it on the next order or withdraw it.

What happens to a crypto payment, step by step

1. You choose an amount

Enter the Dalasi figure you want in the wallet. The app quotes the crypto amount.

2. The rate locks

You have 60 seconds to send. After that the quote refreshes and nothing has moved.

3. We wait for confirmation

One network confirmation for BTC, standard finality for USDT and USDC.

4. Dalasi lands

The locked Dalasi figure is credited, even if the market moved while you waited.

You never hold a crypto balance on Nufaa, cannot withdraw in crypto, and cannot be paid cashback in crypto. Crypto is a way to get Dalasi into the wallet, nothing more.

Straight answers

The questions
people actually ask.

Is this borrowing?

No. You can only spend money already in your wallet. There is no credit line, no repayment and no interest in either direction.

Who pays for the cashback?

The seller, out of the commission they already agreed to pay. They give up margin to win an order they might otherwise lose to the market down the road.

Can I be paid cashback in crypto?

No. Cashback is always Dalasi. Crypto only exists on Nufaa as a way to fund a wallet, and it is converted before your balance changes.

What if an order goes wrong?

Raise it in the app before the collection window closes. Funds have not reached the seller yet, so a refund goes straight back to your wallet in Dalasi.

Does cashback expire?

No. Once credited it is ordinary balance. There is no points system, no tier and no expiry date to keep track of.

Why do I have to do KYC?

Because Nufaa is a registered company in the United Kingdom and The Gambia handling customer funds. Anti-money-laundering rules in both places require a verified identity behind every account.

Is my balance protected?

Customer funds are held separately from the company's own money, but Nufaa is not a bank and no government deposit guarantee applies. Keep only what you plan to spend.

Can I sell as well as buy?

Yes, from the same verified account. Choose "buy and sell" on the KYC form and we will send you the seller onboarding steps.
