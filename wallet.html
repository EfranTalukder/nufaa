Keeping it safe

What protects
the balance.

And, just as importantly, what does not. We would rather you know the limits of this before you put money in than after.

Funds held separately

Customer balances sit in a separate account from the company's own money and are not used to run the business.

Verified accounts only

Every wallet is attached to a KYC-checked person with a document on file, which is what makes fraud traceable.

Withdrawals to your own details

Money leaves only to a number or account registered on your verified profile. Changing those triggers a review.

No deposit guarantee

Nufaa is not a bank. No government scheme in the UK or The Gambia insures your balance. Keep in the wallet only what you plan to spend.

Nufaa operates a retail marketplace and a closed-loop wallet used to pay for goods bought on that marketplace. It is not a bank, does not take deposits, does not lend, and does not pay interest. Cashback is a discount funded by merchant margin, is variable by category, and can change. Wallet balances are not covered by any deposit guarantee scheme in the United Kingdom or The Gambia. Crypto is accepted only as a funding method and is converted to Dalasi before your balance updates.
