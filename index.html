What we do

Nufaa Technologies Limited is a digital services and customer-loyalty technology platform. The company provides access to essential digital products such as airtime, mobile data bundles, utility top-ups, and other merchant-supplied services. Nufaa also operates a promotional rewards programme funded by merchant commissions. The company does not hold customer funds or provide regulated financial services.

Services

Essential digital products, supplied by our merchant partners.

Every product below is sourced from a merchant and delivered to you digitally. Nufaa is the technology layer that connects the two.

Airtime top-ups

Credit added to a mobile number, delivered as soon as the order is confirmed.

Mobile data bundles

Data packages in a range of sizes, applied directly to the number you give us.

Utility top-ups

Prepaid energy and other household utility credit, handled through our suppliers.

Merchant services

Other digital products supplied by partner merchants and listed as they are added.

Rewards programme

A promotional discount, funded by merchants.

When you order through Nufaa, the merchant pays us a commission. Part of that commission is returned to you as a promotional reward. It is a marketing discount, not interest, not an investment, and not a financial product.
