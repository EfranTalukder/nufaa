Two ways to pay, one currency out

Pay with Dalasi or crypto.
Cashback is always Dalasi.

Every price on Nufaa is quoted in Gambian Dalasi. If you pay with crypto, we lock a conversion rate for 60 seconds and settle the merchant in Dalasi — so nobody in the chain carries the volatility, and your reward lands in the currency you actually spend.

then the wallet opens.

Because Nufaa is registered in the United Kingdom and The Gambia and moves real money, we verify every account before it can top up, shop or sell. It takes about five minutes and you only do it once.

Open the KYC form How we store your documents

What you will need

  • Your full legal name and date of birth

    Exactly as printed on your identity document.

  • A passport number or Gambian NID number

    Driving licence and voter's card are accepted as alternatives.

  • A photo of that document

    Both sides for an NID card. All four corners visible, no glare.

  • A photo of your face

    A plain selfie, so we can match it against the document.

Where we are

Built in order,
not all at once.

Dalasi first, everywhere. Crypto sits on top as a way to fund a wallet, not as a currency you have to understand.

  1. Now Step 1

    Registration and Dalasi trading

    Company registration completed in the United Kingdom and The Gambia. Selling starts with payments taken in Dalasi and cashback paid in Dalasi.

  2. In build Step 2

    Everything inside the app

    Top up, buy, list, sell, withdraw — the whole loop in one place, priced in Dalasi from the first screen to the receipt.

  3. Next Step 3

    Customers pay with USDT, USDC or BTC at checkout. Conversion happens before confirmation, and the seller still receives Dalasi.

  4. Planned Step 4

    Diaspora ordering

    Pay from the UK for a basket collected in Banjul or Brikama, with the cashback credited to the family member who picks it up.

Why this exists

“Families here already spend the money. The problem was never the spending — it was that none of it ever came back.”

Nufaa started from a simple observation about how households in the Greater Banjul area buy: in cash, at whatever price the day gives, with no record and no reward. A marketplace that quotes in Dalasi, keeps a receipt, and pays part of the margin back to the buyer changes the arithmetic of an ordinary week.

Nufaa operates a retail marketplace and a closed-loop wallet used to pay for goods bought on that marketplace. It is not a bank, does not take deposits, does not lend, and does not pay interest. Cashback is a discount funded by merchant margin, is variable by category, and can change. Wallet balances are not covered by any deposit guarantee scheme in the United Kingdom or The Gambia. Crypto is accepted only as a funding method and is converted to Dalasi before your balance updates.
