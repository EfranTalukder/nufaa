Why we ask for this

Anti-money-laundering rules in both jurisdictions we are registered in require a verified residential address before an account can hold or move value. We may later ask for a utility bill or bank statement dated within the last three months.

Launch updates

Nufaa operates a retail marketplace and a closed-loop wallet used to pay for goods bought on that marketplace. It is not a bank, does not take deposits, does not lend, and does not pay interest. Cashback is a discount funded by merchant margin, is variable by category, and can change. Wallet balances are not covered by any deposit guarantee scheme in the United Kingdom or The Gambia. Crypto is accepted only as a funding method and is converted to Dalasi before your balance updates.
