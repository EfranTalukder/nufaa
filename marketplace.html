Reading the rates

Why a mattress
beats a bag of rice.

Cashback comes out of the seller's margin, not out of thin air. Staples turn over fast on a tighter margin, so they sit at the bottom of the band. Household goods and fabric carry more room, so sellers push toward the top of it to win the order.

Launch updates

Nufaa operates a retail marketplace and a closed-loop wallet used to pay for goods bought on that marketplace. It is not a bank, does not take deposits, does not lend, and does not pay interest. Cashback is a discount funded by merchant margin, is variable by category, and can change. Wallet balances are not covered by any deposit guarantee scheme in the United Kingdom or The Gambia. Crypto is accepted only as a funding method and is converted to Dalasi before your balance updates. Products, prices and rates shown are illustrative of the launch catalogue.
