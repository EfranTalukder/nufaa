In short. Nufaa is a marketplace where you buy and sell goods priced in Gambian Dalasi, using a wallet you fund yourself. Cashback is a discount paid for by the seller, credited in Dalasi. We are not a bank, we do not lend, and we do not pay interest. Every account must be verified before it can move money. This summary is not part of the agreement — the sections below are.

1. Who we are

Nufaa is operated by two related companies: one registered in the United Kingdom, which owns and runs the platform, and one registered in The Gambia, which operates the marketplace, contracts with sellers and agents, takes Dalasi payments and settles sellers. In these terms, "Nufaa", "we" and "us" mean whichever of those entities is providing the service in question.

Company registration is not financial regulation. Nufaa does not hold a banking, deposit-taking or lending licence in either jurisdiction, and does not present itself as holding one.

2. What Nufaa is, and what it is not

Nufaa provides:

  • an online marketplace where independent sellers list goods and services priced in Gambian Dalasi;
  • a closed-loop wallet, denominated in Dalasi, used to pay for those goods;
  • a cashback mechanism through which part of the seller's commission is returned to the buyer in Dalasi.

Nufaa does not provide:

  • credit, loans, buy-now-pay-later or any other form of borrowing;
  • interest, profit share, yield, staking or investment returns on any balance;
  • a general-purpose payment account, a bank account or a money remittance service;
  • custody of cryptoassets, or any facility to hold, trade or withdraw cryptoassets.

Money in your wallet is not a deposit. It is not protected by the Financial Services Compensation Scheme in the United Kingdom, by any scheme operated by the Central Bank of The Gambia, or by any equivalent arrangement anywhere else. If Nufaa were to become insolvent, you could lose the balance. Keep in the wallet only what you intend to spend.

3. Eligibility and verification

To open an account you must:

  • be at least 18 years old;
  • be acting for yourself, not on behalf of an undisclosed third party;
  • complete identity verification (KYC) and pass our checks.

Verification requires your full legal name, date of birth, residential address, and a valid identity document — a passport, a Gambian national identity (NID) card, a driving licence or a voter's card. You must give us the document number, a photograph of the document, and a photograph of your face so we can match the two. We may ask for further evidence, such as a recent utility bill or bank statement, before or after approving an account.

We may refuse an application, and we do not have to give a reason. Where the law permits us to explain, we will.

4. Your account

Your account is personal to you. Do not share your credentials, let anyone else transact on your account, or open more than one account without our agreement. You are responsible for everything done through your account.

Tell us immediately if you think someone else has access, or if your registered phone number or email changes. Changes to the mobile money number or bank account used for withdrawals will trigger a security review before they take effect.

5. The Dalasi wallet

Your wallet holds a balance denominated in Gambian Dalasi (GMD). It exists to pay for goods and services on the Nufaa marketplace and to receive cashback, refunds and, for sellers, settlement.

  • The balance carries no interest and no return of any kind.
  • Customer funds are held separately from Nufaa's own operating money.
  • Starting limits apply to every newly verified account and may be raised or lowered at our discretion.
  • We may place a temporary hold on a balance where we are required to investigate a transaction.

6. Topping up, including with crypto

You may fund your wallet by mobile money, bank transfer from an account in your own name, cash handed to a listed Nufaa agent, or by sending supported cryptoassets.

Cryptoasset funding

  • Supported assets at launch are USDT, USDC and BTC. We may add or remove assets at any time.
  • You will be shown the Dalasi amount and the rate before you send anything. That quote is held for a short period, currently 60 seconds.
  • If you send within the quoted period, you receive the quoted Dalasi amount, even if the market moves while the transfer confirms.
  • If you send after the quote expires, send the wrong asset, or use an unsupported network, we will credit at the rate applicable when the funds are identified, or return them less costs where return is possible.
  • Cryptoassets are converted on receipt. You never hold a crypto balance with Nufaa, and you cannot withdraw in crypto.

Cryptoasset transfers are irreversible. We cannot recover funds sent to an incorrect address or on an unsupported network.

7. Buying on the marketplace

Sellers on Nufaa are independent businesses. Your contract for the goods is with the seller. Nufaa provides the marketplace, takes the payment, holds it until the order is confirmed, and settles the seller.

  • Prices shown include the seller's price in Dalasi. Any delivery or collection charge is shown before you confirm.
  • You can only spend the balance already in your wallet. Orders that exceed your balance will not go through.
  • Once you confirm, the amount leaves your available balance and is held until the order completes.
  • Orders complete when the seller marks them delivered or collected, or automatically when the collection window closes.

8. Cashback

Cashback is a discount funded out of the commission the seller pays Nufaa on a completed sale. It is not a reward for holding a balance, not a return on money, and not interest.

  • The rate is set per listing by the seller, within limits we set, and is shown before you pay. The rate displayed at the moment of purchase is the rate that applies.
  • Cashback is credited in Gambian Dalasi to the wallet used to pay. It is never paid in cryptoassets or any other currency.
  • Cashback is released when the order is confirmed complete, not when it is placed.
  • Once credited it is ordinary wallet balance. It does not expire and is not held in a separate points scheme.
  • If an order is cancelled or refunded, any cashback credited on it is reversed.
  • We may withhold or reverse cashback where we reasonably believe an order was placed to generate cashback rather than to buy goods.

9. Selling on the marketplace

Sellers must complete the same verification as buyers, plus any business documents we request. By listing on Nufaa you agree that:

  • your listings are accurate, lawful, and priced in Dalasi;
  • you hold the stock you list, or can obtain it within the stated timeframe;
  • you will honour the cashback rate attached to a listing at the moment a buyer purchases;
  • Nufaa deducts its commission, from which buyer cashback is funded, before settling you;
  • settlement is made in Dalasi to the account registered on your verified profile.

Repeated cancellations, misdescribed goods or failure to fulfil confirmed orders may result in listings being removed and your seller access being withdrawn.

10. Withdrawals

You may withdraw your available balance in Dalasi to a mobile money account or bank account registered in your own name, or collect it in cash from a listed agent. Cashback withdraws on the same terms as any other balance.

Daily and per-transaction limits apply. We may delay a withdrawal while we complete checks required by anti-money-laundering law, and we will tell you when we do, unless we are prohibited from doing so.

11. Fees

  • Buyers pay no fee to open an account, hold a balance, or receive cashback.
  • Sellers pay a commission on completed sales, disclosed in the seller agreement.
  • Third parties — mobile money operators, banks and blockchain networks — may charge their own fees, which are outside our control.
  • Crypto conversion costs are reflected in the rate you are quoted; there is no separate line-item charge.

Where we introduce a new fee, we will give at least 30 days' notice.

12. Prohibited use

You must not use Nufaa to:

  • launder money, finance terrorism, or evade sanctions;
  • buy or sell anything unlawful, counterfeit, stolen, or restricted under Gambian or UK law;
  • move value on behalf of someone else without telling us;
  • place orders that are not genuine purchases, including transactions arranged between a buyer and seller to extract cashback;
  • attempt to access another user's account, or to interfere with the platform.

13. Suspension and closure

We may suspend or close an account where we reasonably suspect a breach of these terms or of the law, where verification cannot be completed or maintained, or where we are required to by a regulator or court.

If we close your account and the balance is lawfully yours, we will return it in Dalasi to a verified account in your name. You may close your account at any time by contacting us, once outstanding orders have completed.

14. Disputes, refunds and cancellations

Raise a problem with an order in the app before the collection window closes. Because funds are held until an order completes, most problems are resolved by returning the amount to your wallet in Dalasi.

  • Refunds are made in Dalasi to the wallet used for the purchase, regardless of how that wallet was funded.
  • Where an order was funded with cryptoassets, the refund is still Dalasi — we do not return cryptoassets.
  • Cashback attached to a refunded order is reversed.
  • If you and the seller cannot agree, Nufaa will decide the outcome on the evidence, and that decision is final within the platform. This does not affect your legal rights.

Subject to that, we are not liable for:

  • the quality, safety, legality or delivery of goods sold by independent sellers, beyond our obligations as marketplace operator;
  • losses caused by you giving incorrect details, including sending cryptoassets to a wrong address or network;
  • losses caused by mobile money operators, banks or blockchain networks;
  • indirect or consequential loss, or loss of profit.

We may change these terms. For changes that materially affect you, we will give at least 30 days' notice by email or in the app. Continuing to use Nufaa after a change takes effect means you accept it. If you do not, you may close your account and withdraw your balance.

Marketplace transactions, seller settlement and the agent network are governed by the laws of The Gambia, and the courts of The Gambia have jurisdiction over disputes arising from them.
