Legal

Privacy policy

Last updated 18 August 2026 UK GDPR and Gambian data protection law

On this page

In short. To open a Nufaa account you have to prove who you are, which means giving us a passport or NID number, a photo of that document and a photo of your face. We use it to verify you, to meet anti-money-laundering law, and for nothing else. We do not sell personal data. You can ask to see, correct or erase what we hold, subject to the records we are legally required to keep.

1. Who controls your data

Nufaa operates through a company registered in the United Kingdom and a company registered in The Gambia. The UK entity is the controller for platform accounts, identity verification records and website data. The Gambian entity is the controller for marketplace transactions, seller settlement and the agent network. They act as joint controllers where those activities overlap.

Contact for all data protection matters: nufaa.now@hotmail.com.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Identity: full legal name, date of birth, gender, nationality.
  • Contact: mobile number, email address, residential address.
  • Identity document: document type, document number (passport number or NID number), issuing country, expiry date.
  • Photographs: an image of your identity document, and a photograph of your face used to match against it.
  • Financial context: expected monthly spend, source of funds, whether you are a politically exposed person, and whether the account will buy, sell or both.
  • Seller information: where you trade, what you stock, and the settlement account you nominate.
  • Correspondence: anything you send us through the contact form, email or WhatsApp.

Information we generate or receive

  • Transaction records: top-ups, orders, cashback, refunds, withdrawals and settlements, all denominated in Dalasi.
  • Crypto funding records: the asset, network, transaction hash, amount received and the rate applied at conversion.
  • Device and usage data: IP address, device type, app version, and how you move through the site or app.
  • Verification results from the providers who check your document, and screening results against sanctions and PEP lists.

3. Identity documents and photographs

The KYC form asks for your passport number or Gambian NID number, an image of that document, and a photograph of your face. This is the most sensitive data we hold, and it is handled accordingly:

  • Documents are sent over an encrypted (TLS) connection.
  • During this launch phase, applications are delivered by our forms processor, FormSubmit, to a monitored Nufaa mailbox, and reviewed by hand. We are moving to a dedicated verification platform before general release, and will update this policy when we do.
  • Access is limited to the people who review applications.
  • They are never used for marketing, never sold, and never shown to sellers, agents or other users.
  • Where facial comparison is carried out by an automated provider, it is done to confirm the photograph matches the document, not to build a biometric profile for any other purpose.

If facial matching involves processing biometric data under UK GDPR, we rely on the substantial public interest condition for preventing money laundering, and on your explicit consent given on the KYC form. You may withdraw that consent, but we would then be unable to operate an account for you.

4. Why we use it, and on what basis

  • To provide the service — opening your account, running the wallet, processing orders, crediting cashback, settling sellers. Basis: performance of a contract.
  • To verify identity and prevent financial crime — KYC, sanctions and PEP screening, transaction monitoring. Basis: legal obligation, and substantial public interest for any special category data.
  • To keep the platform safe — detecting fraud, cashback abuse and account takeover. Basis: legitimate interests.
  • To support you — answering questions and handling disputes. Basis: performance of a contract, and legitimate interests.
  • To send launch and service updates — Basis: consent for marketing email, which you can withdraw at any time; legitimate interests for essential service notices.
  • To meet accounting and regulatory duties — Basis: legal obligation.

5. Who we share it with

  • FormSubmit (Devro LABS), the processor that delivers form submissions from this website to our mailbox.
  • Identity verification providers, where used, to check your document and confirm the photograph matches it.
  • Payment partners — mobile money operators, banks and the processor that converts cryptoassets — with only what is needed to move the money.
  • Sellers and agents, who receive the minimum needed to fulfil an order: your name, the delivery or collection point, and the order itself. They never receive your identity documents, document numbers or balance.
  • Professional advisers — auditors and lawyers, under confidentiality.
  • Regulators, law enforcement and courts, where we are legally required to disclose. In some cases the law prevents us from telling you.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with advertising networks.

6. Sending data between countries

Because Nufaa operates in both the United Kingdom and The Gambia, personal data moves between the two. The Gambia is not covered by a UK adequacy decision, so transfers out of the UK are made under the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or the addendum to the EU standard contractual clauses, together with additional safeguards including encryption and access controls. A copy of the transfer mechanism is available on request.

7. How long we keep it

  • Identity documents and verification records: five years after your account closes, which is the retention period anti-money-laundering law expects in both jurisdictions.
  • Transaction records: at least six years, for tax and accounting purposes.
  • Support correspondence: two years after the matter is closed.
  • Marketing consent records: until you withdraw consent, plus two years to evidence that you had consented.
  • Website analytics: fourteen months.

When a retention period ends, records are deleted or irreversibly anonymised.

8. How we protect it

  • Encryption in transit and at rest, with identity documents held in a separate restricted store.
  • Role-based access, so ordinary support staff cannot open verification records.
  • Access logging and periodic review of who has looked at what.
  • Multi-factor authentication on internal systems.

No system is completely secure. If a breach is likely to result in a high risk to you, we will tell you without undue delay, and notify the relevant regulator within the required timeframe.

9. Your rights

Subject to the exemptions in applicable law, you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct anything that is wrong or out of date;
  • erase data we no longer have a lawful reason to keep;
  • restrict or object to certain processing;
  • provide your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

Anti-money-laundering law limits some of these rights. We cannot erase a verification record inside its statutory retention period, even at your request. We respond to requests within one month, and will tell you if we need longer.

10. Cookies and similar technology

This website uses only what it needs to work, plus aggregate analytics to understand which pages are read. We do not use advertising cookies and do not build profiles for targeting. Fonts and the styling framework are loaded from third-party content delivery networks, which will see your IP address as part of serving those files.

11. Children

Nufaa is not for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we will close it and delete the data, apart from anything we are legally required to retain.

12. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our practices change, and change the date at the top. For material changes we will tell you by email or in the app before they take effect.

13. Contact and complaints

Write to nufaa.now@hotmail.com with any data protection question or request. We acknowledge within two working days.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office in the United Kingdom at ico.org.uk, or to the relevant supervisory authority in The Gambia. We would rather you came to us first so we can put it right.

This policy is provided in English. Where a translation is offered for convenience, the English version governs.
